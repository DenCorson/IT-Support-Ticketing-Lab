Ticket 2 - User Account Locked

## Problem

Received troubleticket states that user, Ned Kevin, is locked out of their acount.

## Troubleshooting

I located the the user's account in Active Directory, and verified that their account was locked out, as ticket suggested.

[Locked account verified in Active Directory] ([images/ticket-02/02-ad-account-locked.png]([https://github.com/DenCorson/IT-Support-Ticketing-Lab/blob/4908e97225152bd4c3f4540ff57e9ab6a6eb827d/images/ticket-02/01-ticket-submitted.png](https://github.com/DenCorson/IT-Support-Ticketing-Lab/blob/4908e97225152bd4c3f4540ff57e9ab6a6eb827d/images/ticket-02/02-ad-account-locked.png))

From there I selected their account and unlocked it. Afterwards, I had notified to submitter that the account for Ned Kevin was unlocked any they would need to input their old password to login.

##Resolution

User was able to login to their workstation.

Ticket was resolved and closed.
