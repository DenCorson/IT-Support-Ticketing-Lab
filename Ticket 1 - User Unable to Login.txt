# Ticket 1 - User Unable to Login

## Problem

User, Oliver Johnson, submitted troubleticket stating that they were Having issues with logging into their account, after password reset.

## Troubleshooting

Before I began checking the user's account, I asked them for a description of what the error was displaying to them, I was then told that the error said "The password is incorrect. Try again." 

From there I went to the user's account, located in their respective Organization Unit (OU) and reset their password with a temporary password, after the reset I notified them that I was going to send their temporary password through a secure method.

## Resolution

The user confirmed that they were able to login to their workstation with the provided temporary password and were prompted to change it with their login. 

Ticket was resolved and closed.
